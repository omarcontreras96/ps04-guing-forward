Pico Space Pro Leaks Ahead of Planned 2026 Launch, Including External & Internal Images

Scott Hayden
October 12, 2026
3

Regulatory filings in Japan may have inadvertently leaked Pico’s next flagship headset in full—inside and out.

As first spotted by UploadVR, images of Pico’s Vison Pro competitor ‘Space Pro’ have leaked ahead of its planned Q4 2026 launch.

Filed under certification number 202-JWM032, the images are publicly available through Japan’s Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC), which, like the US FCC, oversees the certification of electronic and radio communications equipment.

We’ve extracted them from both available PDFs containing photos of both the external and internals of the device; click to enlarge each photo.

Externals

Internals

Widely considered Pico’s answer to Apple Vision Pro, Space Pro was initially meant to debut earlier this month, however the ByteDance subsidiary cancelled the event and postponed its reveal and release date.

Previously codenamed ‘Project Swan’, Pico Space Pro doesn’t have an official release date yet beyond the expected Q4 2026 launch window, although the device has also surfaced in FCC filings in addition to the Japanese equivalent, suggesting it could be any day now.
