Meta says its new Muse Wonder agents check in before doing anything important. Hands-on reports and new research suggest that asking first isn't the same as getting it right. Agents held up in a bounded test where the outcome was checked, and stumbled when they acted on messy personal context. We look at what holds up, what doesn't, and how thin the evidence still is.
