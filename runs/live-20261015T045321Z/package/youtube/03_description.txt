Is talking to AI, the way HAL worked in 2001, the best way to use it? This month's evidence suggests conversation is a strong way to hand off work and a weak way to check it. A blogger's voice-built feature, two HCI studies, a year with Alexa Plus and new research on trust all point the same way. Use voice or chat as a dispatcher, and keep a visual or structured view for anything you need to own.
